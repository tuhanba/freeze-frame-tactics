Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

"Pass Shorts" is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Freeze Frame Tactics. It is not offered to other users.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube upload (youtube.upload): to upload the channel's own videos and set their publish time.
  • YouTube read-only (youtube.readonly): to confirm the target channel and check the status of uploaded videos.
  • YouTube Analytics read-only (yt-analytics.readonly): to read view counts and similar statistics for the channel's own videos.

How data is used and stored

The app runs on the owner's own computer. The OAuth refresh token is stored in Windows Credential Manager on that computer. Video status and statistics are stored in a local database on the same computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data about viewers or other YouTube users is collected.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data is deleted when the app is removed from the owner's computer.
